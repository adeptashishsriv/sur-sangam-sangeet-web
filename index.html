Our Story

About Sur Sangam

॥ ॐ ॥
🪕

A Legacy of Music & Devotion

Sur Sangam Sangeet Kala Kendra was founded with a single, profound vision — to preserve the ancient art of Indian classical music and make it accessible to every aspiring musician.

Rooted in the Guru-Shishya Parampara, our institute blends traditional pedagogy with modern teaching methods, ensuring that students not only learn technique but deeply understand the emotional and spiritual essence of music.

Our students have gone on to perform on national stages, participate in prestigious competitions, and carry the torch of Indian musical heritage across the world.

Affiliated Programme

Prayag Sangeet Samiti Diplomas

॥ ॐ ॥

Sur Sangam is an affiliated centre of Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Prayagraj, UP — one of India's most respected music examination bodies. Our students can appear for the following recognised diploma examinations.

Affiliated by Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Prayagraj, UP (Est. 1926)
Level 1
🎓

Junior Diploma

प्रारम्भिक डिप्लोमा

The entry-level certification for students beginning their formal journey in Indian classical music. Covers basic theory, notation, ragas and rhythmic exercises.

  • Foundation of swara & taal
  • Introduction to notation (swarlipi)
  • Basic ragas & compositions
  • Theory & practical examination
Enquire Now
Level 2
🎓

Senior Diploma

वरिष्ठ डिप्लोमा

Building on the Junior Diploma, this programme deepens musical understanding with intermediate-level ragas, taal, and performance skills.

  • Intermediate raga study
  • Advanced taal & layakari
  • Bandish & composition analysis
  • Solo & accompaniment practice
Enquire Now
Most Sought
Level 3
🏅

Prabhakar

प्रभाकर

An advanced diploma recognising substantial mastery. Students develop deep raga understanding, complex compositions and performance readiness.

  • Advanced ragas & bandishes
  • Detailed music theory
  • Concert-level performance skills
  • Music history & aesthetics
Enquire Now
Level 4
🏆

Praveen

प्रवीण

The highest diploma awarded by Prayag Sangeet Samiti, equivalent to a graduate-level qualification. Marks the student as a full-fledged classical artist.

  • Complete classical mastery
  • Research & musicology
  • Teaching & guru capability
  • Nationally recognised credential
